# Part B - Option 2 - visionOS App (15 marks)

Put your **visionOS spatial app** Xcode project in this folder if you chose Option 2.

Design an immersive, spatial computing app for Apple Vision Pro. Ideally this extends your Part A NurseryConnect app, for example an immersive virtual nursery tour for prospective parents, a spatial dashboard for Setting Managers, or an interactive 3D meal planning tool for Catering Staff. You may propose a different topic within the childcare or education domain if you can show enough depth and justify the choice. Go beyond a flat UI recreated in 3D.

## Required deliverables

1. Working prototype using spatial UI elements or 3D assets (committed here)
2. Slide deck, 6 to 8 slides, presenting concept, target audience, and use of spatial computing (add to `Report/` or here)
3. Demo video or screen recording, 1 to 2 minutes (link in `Demo/README.md`)
4. Learning reflection, 1 to 2 pages, referencing at least 3 curated resources or labs you followed, such as WWDC sessions, Apple Labs, or Develop in Swift tutorials (include in your report)
